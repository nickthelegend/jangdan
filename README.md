# Jangdan (장단)

*Jangdan* is Korean for a rhythmic cycle.

Raise your hands and your room becomes a concert hall. Four sections of a light orchestra take their places on your real
walls and table. Your right hand sets the tempo, your left sets the dynamics, and every note washes over the surface it
plays from. The size of your room sets the reverb.
