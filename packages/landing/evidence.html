The room decides the hall.

Placement, light, sound, and the numbers from the emulator.

Where each section sits

Detected planes have no labels. Jangdan reads orientation and height: left wall for strings, right wall for winds, the far wall or the ceiling for brass, the table for the janggu.

Each wash is drawn on that plane’s own polygon, so the colour stops at the real edge. An HRTF source sits at the centroid. The bounding box sets a generated impulse response: a small office stays dry, a loft rings.

A missing wall gets a paper banner, and the banner says so.

Gaze, honestly

Quest 3 has no eye tracking, so a cue is a hand ray and a pinch. On Meta VR Glasses, look at the section and pinch. Both hit the same targets. Listen mode plays the concert while your hands only paint light. Ghost replays your last take.

Run it

Quest 3 or 3S, hand tracking on, Space Setup done. Open the page in the Meta Quest Browser, tap Enter the hall, sit down, raise your right hand.

Same Wi-Fi: the address from npx iwsdk dev status. Over USB: adb reverse tcp:8081 tcp:8081, then https://localhost:8081/. The README has the certificate note. No public URL yet.
